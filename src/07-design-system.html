Spacing and shape

Base unit 8px. Sections 56px apart. Content width 1180px, left aligned. Pills fully round, inputs and buttons 8px, table and drawer 12px. 1px borders, no shadows, no gradients.

Motion

One moment only: the mood bar fills while batches finish. Everything else answers a click: pill fill, drawer open, copy confirmed.

Avoid

Emoji, icons used as decoration, all-caps labels, purple or glow effects, identical cards everywhere, dark mode in v1, marketing words.
